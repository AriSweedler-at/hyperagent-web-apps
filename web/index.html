Ari's web apps

Single-file browser games. Nothing to install — open one on your phone and play.

🃏 Gin Rummy

Two-player Gin Rummy. Play online with a 4-letter room code, pass-and-play on one phone, or just keep score for a game with real cards.

🎲 Fidice

One-cup liar's dice, Kezar Lake rules.

⚅⚄ Sheshbesh

Backgammon, portes or Western rules, on one phone passed between two players.

🃏 Briscola

The Italian trick-taking game for two, three or four, with a running score and Italian card packs: online with a room code, or on one phone passed around the table.
